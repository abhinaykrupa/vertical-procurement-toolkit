# SourceClub case study — original deliverables

This directory contains the original case-study materials submitted for the **Head of AI Powered Operations, Systems & RevOps** role at SourceClub (a dental Group Purchasing Organization), May 2026.

The case study is the **origin story** of this toolkit, not its current scope. The toolkit at the project root is the vertical-agnostic generalization of the dental-specific engine built here.

## Why this is still here

A few reasons it's useful even if you don't care about the SourceClub story:

1. **It shows how the architecture was justified to a business audience** — `SUBMISSION.md` translates 3-stage matching, UOM normalization, and confidence routing into "0.4 FTE freed = $40K/yr saved" language.
2. **The roadmap-prioritization framework generalizes** — the dependency-vs-urgency sequencing thesis in Assignment 3 applies to any small-team technology roadmap.
3. **The strategic-addendum competitive teardown** — Synergy / Alara / Vetcove / pricing models — is a reusable template for thinking about positioning in any vertical procurement market.

## Outcome

The interview ultimately didn't result in an offer. The most likely guess (no specific reason given) is compensation expectations. The work was generalized and open-sourced after the fact, on the principle that good architecture is worth more when others can use it than when it sits in a private repo.
